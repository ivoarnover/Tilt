How to play

One player holds the phone against their forehead, screen facing everyone else. The others describe the word without saying it. The holder guesses.

Clue rules

Relay

A 90-second round where the phone changes foreheads every 30 seconds without stopping the clock. One score for the whole team.

Steals

If a team passes and another team shouts the answer, that team gets the point. Add steals on the scorecard.
